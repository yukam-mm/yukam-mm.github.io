## Purpose

From January 2027 I join Nora Martin's group at the Centre for Genomic Regulation in Barcelona for a research traineeship. The group studies genotype-phenotype maps computationally, and the project asks how environmental changes alter such a map.

## Why this project

A fermentation is an environment that changes hour by hour, and a production strain is a genotype chosen for how it behaves in that environment. I want to understand the general theory of how genotype, environment and phenotype relate, and bring it back to strain and process design.
